# Channel context (what you need for caption and tint judgment)

- **Audience:** Indian PC/mobile gamers on a budget. Free or cheap games, low-spec ("potato PC") lists, single-player story picks, odd/realistic curiosities.
- **Language:** Hinglish. Hindi sentence spine, English gaming words stay English. Captions are romanized (`roman`), never Devanagari. Addresses the viewer as "tum", casual, one gamer to another.
- **Tone:** understated and honest, not hype. That's why sarcasm and deadpan irony are the moments that get the B&W tint. Plain enthusiasm doesn't.
- **Structure:** hook (often a physical stunt on camera) → what the game is + why it matters → **game name revealed after the description** → verdict → CTA "comment <keyword>" (single step).
- **Yellow keyword choice:** the word carrying the claim or the surprise (the game name, "free", a number, the twist word). One per line, max.
